Guardrails

  • Targets outside covered lineages or below 5% observability abstain.
  • CD3E, CD79A and MS4A1 use leave-target-out lineage annotation in both PBMC cohorts.
  • The 313 duplicated 10x barcode sequences in GSE96583 are resolved condition-specifically and checked one-to-one.
  • No protein-aware control benchmark is claimed because ADT/control overlap is zero.

Current blockers

  • paired_tissue_validation: No independent paired inflamed/uninflamed tissue cohort has been converted under frozen rules.
  • control_overlapping_surface_protein: The available GSE157857 ADT panel has no benchmark-control target overlap.
  • confirmatory_target_holdout_coverage: All three preassigned positive holdout targets abstain in GSE96583 at the frozen 5% observability threshold.
